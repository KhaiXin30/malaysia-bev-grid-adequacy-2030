AUTOMOTIVE SECTOR LANDSCAPE AND LINKAGES TO
GOVERNMENT POLICES


Ladies and Gentlemen,


  5.   Malaysia's appeal to global automotive manufacturers remains
       robust. Malaysia has a dynamic automotive industry which support
       over 700,000 workers in its ecosystem, accounting for more than
       4.4% of our workforce. . As an ecosystem, the automotive industry
       contributes around 4% of Malaysia's GDP. By the year 2030 – our
       target is to achieve RM104.2 billion in GDP contribution with a total
       production volume of 1.47 million vehicle units.


  6.
       Last year the Malaysian automotive industry achieved production
       output of over 700,000 vehicles , due to strong demand post-
       pandemic. In 2023, Malaysia’s vehicle sales also registered strong
  growth, where for the first ten months of the year, sales increased
  by 11.6% to almost 647,000 units.[1]


7. Presently, we have more than 30 manufacturers and vehicle
  assemblers who benefit from Malaysia's strong automotive
  ecosystem. The presence of these manufacturers has also had a
  positive spill over impact on the domestic supply chain,
  represented by the more than 600 parts and components makers
  in the country.


8. In growing this sector further, clear national policies are already in
  place which encapsulate strategies, initiatives, and have shaped
  the trajectory of the automotive industry. Malaysia intends to
  transform the industry to greater heights, capitalizing on emerging
  global trends, supply chain disruptions, the current geopolitical
  landscape, digitalization, and ESG considerations.


9. With the launch of the New Industrial Master Plan (NIMP) 2030,
  our mission is clear - Push for Net Zero. This is a key growth driver
  towards decarbonizing Malaysia’s manufacturing industry, and as
  an important component in our holistic efforts to address climate
  change, striving for a net-zero carbon future. Under one of the
  Action Plans, the Government has also identified EVs as a key
  growth driver.


10.    To accelerate our green and sustainable growth agenda, the
  National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR), launched in August
  this year, we have a set a national target, which is 20% of our
  annual new car sales consists of Electrified Vehicles (xEV) by 2030,
  50% by 2040 and 80% by 2050.


11.    At the same time, MITI’s commitment to bring Malaysia to
  the forefront of the electric vehicle revolution can be seen through
  the National Automotive Policy or NAP2020. The NAP provides the
  local automotive industry with a clear direction for future
  preparations, through the development of Next Generation
  Vehicles (NxGV) including EVs that are energy efficient and
  environmentally friendly, with technological intelligence to support
  the smarter, more efficient and safer vehicles.


12.    In terms of market growth, EV markets in Malaysia are seeing
  exponential growth as sales increase by year. To date, Malaysia
  had already recorded more than 100,000 registered xEV. More
  than 12,000 BEV have been registered since 2011 with the number
  of BEV registered this year alone surpassing 9,000 units. The
        Government is optimistic that the positive momentum will
        continue strongly through the end of 2023.

  13.
        Malaysia remains an attractive destination for EV investors.
        Between 2018 and June 2023, MIDA approved 59 projects worth
        RM26.2 billion in the EV and its related ecosystems for vehicle
        assembly, manufacturing parts and charging components. [2]


  14.        We anticipate that the Malaysia Auto Show will be the stage
        for the OEMs, component manufactures and aftersales business to
        showcase the latest advancements in vehicle technologies and
        critical components, marking a significant step towards realizing
        the objectives set forth in the roadmaps.


NOT JUST EVS, BUT OVERALL ENERGY EFFICIENCY


Ladies and Gentlemen,


  15.        As we aim to achieve Malaysia’s net-zero carbon emission by
        2050 as outlined in the Twelfth Malaysia Plan (12MP), we carry an
        even bigger responsibility to ensure all our activities especially
        transportation, is sustainable and greener. Sustainability has
        become a buzzword in every industry, and the automotive sector is
  no exception. It is important that we address the environmental
  impact of our actions and choices.


16.    Transportation is the second biggest contributor of CO2
  emissions after the energy sector. It is also the largest energy user
  in Malaysia, contributing 36.4% of the overall energy consumption
  and hence the Government is committed to drive sustainable
  mobility by reducing energy usage and Green House Gasses (GHG)
  emissions. As such, this year will be an exciting year to grow
  Malaysia’s electromobility industry as EV has been identified as one
  of the technological solutions to reduce carbon emission.


17.    While the government has spoken a lot about electric
  vehicles, it by no means should take away attention from energy
  efficiency in traditional power trains. Even for internal combustion
  engines, the government is continuously incentivising the
  production of energy efficient vehicles (EEVs) in tandem with
  alternative powertrains.



18.    The MAS 2024 is strategically designed to emphasize the
  importance of sustainable practices within the automotive sector.
  By featuring eco-friendly and energy-efficient vehicles, we aim to
   not only promote a green future for the industry but also inspire a
   collective shift towards responsible and sustainable practices, both
   at the industry and consumer levels.




ASPIRATION FOR MALAYSIA AUTOSHOW 2024


 19.    I vividly remember my experience riding in a fully
   autonomous vehicle, developed by our own local talent at the 2023
   edition of the Autoshow. This made me feel very proud, as the
   Autoshow not only showcased the great brands that make up our
   automotive ecosystem, but also showcased the SMEs that have
   worked hard to push boundaries and need a place to showcase
   their talents to the world.


 20.    The strategy for advancing the automotive industry in
   Malaysia revolves around cultivating the nation with future-
   oriented technologies, aiming to transform us an industrial
   powerhouse. This approach centres on high technology, talents,
   emphasizing strategic investments in local supply chain
   development      and   innovative   pursuit   of   new     business
   opportunities. Furthermore, it is anticipated that the automotive
  sector can serve as a catalyst, generating beneficial "spin-offs" that
  extend to the broader Malaysian economy, aligning it with global
  developments.


21.    MAS is about fostering an environment where ideas,
  technologies, and talents converge to shape the future of our
  automotive industry. OEMs, vendors, and aftermarket players
  must forge strong alliances and work closely together.


22.    As we navigate the currents of change, a significant aspect of
  our collective future lies in the realm of EVs. It's not just about
  manufacturing electric vehicles but preparing our talent pool and
  supply chains for the electrified revolution. This shift not only
  underscores    our   commitment       to   sustainability   but   also
  necessitates a synchronized effort across the industry.


23.    NAP 2020 bring a new era of connected mobility, where
  vehicles are not just modes of transportation but interconnected
  hubs of information and technology. In this landscape,
  telecommunication companies are set to play a significant role. The
  integration of IoT (Internet of Things) and seamless connectivity
  will drive this transformation. As we embark on this journey,
    collaboration between automotive and telecommunication sectors
    is not just a recommendation; it's now seemed as a necessity.


  24.    In acknowledging the achievements of MAS 2023 and setting
    the stage for MAS 2024, we must recognize and celebrate the
    talent within our local automotive community. The individuals and
    teams driving the innovations showcased at MAS represent the
    best of Malaysian ingenuity. Their dedication, skill, and unwavering
    commitment contribute significantly to the growth and success of
    our automotive sector. It is therefore, through their efforts that
    Malaysia continues to be a force to be reckoned with in the global
    automotive landscape.

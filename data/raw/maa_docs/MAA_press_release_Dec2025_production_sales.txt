3. Market Performance in December 2025

   Total Industry Volume (TIV) for December 2025 was the highest in history and 25% higher than November 2025
   (72,799 units). The higher TIV is attributed to:

         •   Aggressive year-end promotion campaign by automotive companies to clear stocks.

         •   Rush to purchase CBU BEVs before the expiry of tax incentives and road tax exemption.

         •   Forward purchase in anticipation of the Effective Interest Rate (EIR) vs current Fixed Interest Rate for Hire
             Purchase financing



4. Outlook for January 2026

   Sales is expected to consolidate in January 2026 after three consecutive months of strong performance
